# Front Office Grade Model Validation — 2026-08-21

The desktop Team Overview retains a compact Front Office Evaluation card beside Team Leaders. The visible card hierarchy is unchanged, preserving the decision-first briefing composition; each facet remains a keyboard-accessible details control rather than exposing unsupported calculation text by default.

At 375px, the Team Overview controls, Front Office entry point, and fixed official-MLB ticker fit within the viewport without horizontal overflow. Grade detail controls preserve their existing responsive grid behavior.

The calculation model was strengthened without creating unsupported defense or organization values. Defense is now performance-first: it remains pending or unavailable until comparable Baseball Savant OAA is available, then weights OAA at 80% and verified active fielding coverage at 20%. Baserunning requires eight verified attempts and uses rate-normalized opportunity where team PA coverage permits. Depth requires a dated official MLB Pipeline rank and blends it 65% with verified active-roster coverage. Future Value requires at least two current SKIP prospect rows and combines top-three and top-five organization FV quality percentiles, while disclosing that it is not a complete farm-system depth measure.
